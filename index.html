Selected research

Models that edit without losing the thread.

I work on generative systems that follow complex intent while preserving identity, structure, and context.

AAAI 2027 / Under review First author

Unicorn: Controllable Multi-Shot Video Editing Framework via Shot-wise Keyframe Anchors

The first controllable framework for multi-shot video editing. Unicorn combines shot-aware planning, in-context keyframe editing, and edited-frame anchors, improving the strongest baseline by 8.94% on MultiShotBench.

  • Video editing
  • Shot localization
  • Cross-shot consistency
Manuscript available after review
ACM MM 2026 / Accepted First author

SREdit: A Self-Rewarding Framework with Intrinsically Aligned Critic for Image Editing

Aligns instruction understanding with reward evaluation in one model, reducing semantic drift in reinforcement-learning-based image editing.

  • Image editing
  • Self-rewarding
  • MLLM-DiT
Final paper coming soon
ICML 2026 / Poster First author, equal contribution

FedPDG: Prediction Discrepancy-Guided Data Generation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning

Uses prediction discrepancy to discover missing local data and guide personalized diffusion generation, improving heterogeneous federated learning by up to 3.82%.
